# Scientific Research

## Purpose
Answer science questions with evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and reproducibility.

## When to use
Use this skill when a project needs scientific research as a defined workstream. Combine it with `project-orchestration` for multi-discipline tasks.

## Workflow
1. Translate the question into testable subquestions and required evidence.
2. Prioritize primary scientific publications, official mission data, and institutional sources.
3. Record source title, publisher, date, URL/DOI, method, and relevant limitations.
4. Cross-check consequential claims with independent authoritative sources.
5. Distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis, and established consensus.
6. Report units, uncertainty, time/space coverage, and reproducibility steps.

## Deliverables
Produce fit-for-purpose artifacts such as: source-ledger.md, research-brief.md, reproducibility.md. Do not create paperwork that does not help a decision or implementation.

## Quality gates
- Deliverables are specific to the project, internally consistent, and actionable.
- Assumptions and unknowns are explicitly labeled.
- High-impact risks have mitigations or an owner and acceptance decision.
- Claims are supported by implementation evidence or clearly marked as proposals.
- Review for accessibility, privacy, security, maintainability, and user impact as applicable.

## Safety and integrity
Never fabricate a citation, measurement, source access, or confidence level.
- Ask for clarification only when a missing detail blocks safe or correct work; otherwise state a reasonable assumption.
- Do not expose credentials, personal data, or confidential material in artifacts.
- Do not fabricate external research, test runs, approvals, deployment, or tool outcomes.
- Human approval is required for consequential external actions, production changes, and irreversible operations.

## Handoff
Summarize: objective, decisions, changed/produced artifacts, validation performed, unresolved issues, and next recommended step. Link to artifacts by repository-relative path.
